1. Brand Overview

Clarinvest is an AI-powered stock intelligence platform built on a single belief: that institutional-quality analysis should be accessible to every investor, not just those with Bloomberg terminals. We translate raw financial data into sharp, actionable insights across US, UK and European markets.

Mission
To give every investor the clarity to make confident, informed decisions without the noise.

Position
Clarinvest sits at the intersection of premium fintech and accessible intelligence. The product is sophisticated but never intimidating, analytical but never cold. Think: a world-class analyst, available any time, speaking plain English.

Brand personality

Precise
Every word and number earns its place. No filler, no vague language.

Confident
Clarinvest speaks with authority. It does not hedge unnecessarily.

Accessible
Institutional depth in plain, human language.	Refined
The brand is elegant and considered. Never loud or gimmicky.

Modern
Technology-forward aesthetic. Clean layouts, purposeful motion.

Trustworthy
Consistency in every detail signals reliability. Finance demands it.
 
2. Logo

The Clarinvest identity consists of two elements: the logomark (the four-square symbol) and the logotype (the wordmark). They may be used together or independently, following the rules below.

The logomark
Four rounded squares arranged in a 2x2 grid. Two squares are solid (top-left and bottom-right), two are at 22% opacity (top-right and bottom-left). The diagonal contrast creates visual tension and suggests both duality (bull/bear, light/dark) and structured data.

DARK BACKGROUND	LIGHT BACKGROUND	COLOUR BACKGROUND	AVOID: BUSY BG

The logotype
The wordmark is set in Playfair Display Semibold 600, all-caps, with a letter-spacing of 0.03em. It is always rendered in the foreground text colour of the current background. Never use a colour other than black, white, or the platform's primary text token.

Clear space and minimum size
Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the lettermark square around every side of the logo. Minimum reproduction size: 120px wide for digital, 25mm for print. Below these sizes, use the logomark only (four squares without wordmark).

Logo usage rules
DO
+ Use the logomark on plain, high-contrast backgrounds
+ Pair the logomark with the wordmark in the nav and official documents
+ Use the SVG version for all digital applications
+ Maintain the exact opacity values (100% and 22%) of the four squares	DON'T
- Do not rotate, skew, or distort the logomark
- Do not apply drop shadows or outer glows
- Do not change the opacity ratio of the squares
- Do not place on low-contrast or photographic backgrounds
- Do not recreate the mark in other software
- Do not use the logotype alone without at least a 0.03em letter-spacing

5. Voice and Tone

How Clarinvest writes is as important as how it looks. The brand voice is consistent everywhere. The tone adapts to context.

Brand voice

Sharp, not terse
Get to the point immediately. No warm-up sentences, no filler phrases like 'In order to...' or 'It is important to note that...'

Confident, not arrogant
State conclusions clearly. Do not over-hedge with phrases like 'It could be argued that...' But never claim certainty on market outcomes.

Accessible, not dumbed down
Explain jargon on first use. Write for an intelligent adult who is not a finance professional.	Human, not robotic
The AI analysis should read like a knowledgeable colleague, not a press release or a legal disclaimer.

Precise, not padded
If three words will do, do not use six. Every sentence in a UI label or analysis output should earn its space.

Serious, not cold
Money is a serious topic. The tone is considered and calm, not cheerful or alarmist.

Writing examples

WRITE THIS	NOT THIS
Revenue grew 122% year-on-year, ahead of the sector average by 4x.	We are pleased to report strong revenue growth performance metrics for this period.
NVIDIA's AI accelerator dominance is structural. CUDA lock-in makes switching costly for hyperscalers.	NVIDIA could potentially be considered a dominant player in the AI accelerator space.
PEG of 0.9. Growth is not fully priced in at current levels.	It is worth noting that the PEG ratio may indicate some potential upside opportunity.
Invest with Absolute Clarity	Your trusted AI-powered financial assistant for smarter decisions
Cancel anytime. No setup fees.	You can cancel your subscription at any time without any additional charges or fees!

Punctuation notes
•	Do not use em dashes ( - ). Use a comma, colon, or full stop instead.
•	Do not use exclamation marks in product copy or analysis outputs.
•	Use the serial comma (Oxford comma): 'value, growth, and momentum'.
•	Abbreviate large numbers: 122% not 122.00%, $1.2B not $1,200,000,000.
•	Currency symbols precede the number with no space: £29, not £ 29.

8. Motion and Animation

Motion in Clarinvest serves clarity, not decoration. Every animation has a functional reason. When in doubt, do less.

Principles
•	Subtle: no element should animate in a way that distracts from content.
•	Purposeful: scroll reveals, hover states, and loading transitions communicate state.
•	Fast: UI response transitions are 220ms. Page-level transitions are 700ms maximum.
•	GPU-safe: use only transform and opacity for animations. Never animate background-position, width, height, or filter (they trigger repaints and cause scroll lag on mobile).

Standard values

Button hover lift
translateY(-2px), 220ms ease

Card hover lift
translateY(-5px), 280ms ease

Scroll reveal
opacity 0 to 1 + translateY(22px to 0), 700ms ease, IntersectionObserver 8% threshold	Hero orb drift
transform translate + scale, 18-22s ease-in-out infinite. No CSS filter.

Ticker strip
translateX, 34s linear infinite. Duplicate content for seamless loop.

Floating chips
translateY 0 to -11px, 6s ease-in-out infinite. Staggered delays per chip.

The silver headline gradient
The 'Absolute Clarity' hero text uses a left-to-right silver shimmer on a symmetric gradient (#686868 to #ffffff to #686868). Background-size: 200%. Animation: background-position from 200% to 0% at 3s linear infinite. The symmetric endpoints ensure a seamless loop with no visible restart.
 
9. Legal and Compliance

Every product surface, document, and communication must carry the standard disclaimer. The exact wording is fixed and must not be edited.

Standard disclaimer

For informational purposes only. Not financial advice.
Clarinvest provides AI-generated analysis for informational purposes only. Nothing on this platform constitutes financial advice, investment advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Always conduct your own due diligence and consult a qualified financial adviser before making investment decisions.

The short form (footer, small print) is: 'For informational purposes only. Not financial advice.'
The full form is required on the landing page about section, any email communication, and any document that includes market data or analysis.

Disclaimer placement rules
•	Landing page footer: short form, always visible
•	Analysis pages: short form in the page header or above the chart
•	Email newsletters: full form in the email footer
•	Exported reports or PDF outputs: full form on the cover page
•	Do not remove or abbreviate beyond the approved short form
